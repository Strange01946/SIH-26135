# Phase 30.9 government MIS coverage

The dashboard at `/analytics` uses the Q01–Q11 numbering in `database/phase-18-dashboard-analytical-queries.sql`. The reporting period is the financial year of enrollment. Other common filters are scheme, sector, state, district, and recorded gender. Geography uses the trainee’s location. When a district is selected, its parent state is selected with it. Q07–Q11 link to the filtered enrollment cohort when a filter is applied and include unlinked records in the all-time, unfiltered nationwide view where the query grain allows it.

**Government MIS access** follows the account’s recorded jurisdiction. Nationwide super admins, government admins and national analysts can report across India. State administrators can report within their assigned state, and district officers within their assigned district. The backend applies these boundaries even when a request supplies no geographic filter and rejects filters outside the assignment. The Executive overview loads Q01, Q02, and Q06 together for the selected filters. The comparison tabs load on demand.

The Social Category control is disabled because the Phase 27 schema has no authoritative category field. The filter API identifies this as unsupported. The scheme catalog records an administering organization and budget, with no funding split. The comparison names each administrative group explicitly.

Government MIS displays exact counts, rates and applicable wage aggregates, including cohorts with one to four trainees. Rates and wages show No data when the required observations are missing. Specialized national reporting and exports use their separate read policies.

The tests in `frontend/src/__tests__/analytics/misDashboard.test.tsx` check the shared filters across all endpoints, omission of suppressed geometry, and sorting of released values. Backend coverage includes `AnalyticsMultiDimensionalFilteringTest`, `EconomicOutcomeDashboardIntegrationTest`, `InstitutionalAnalyticsIntegrationTest`, `DashboardTenancyAndRoleAuthorizationTest`, and `AnalyticsStatisticalDisclosureControlTest`. The live checks in the table used Spring Boot, Vite, and Chromium against `SIH26135_TEST`, with both larger cohorts and cohorts of one to four trainees.

The locally bundled map adapts the 36-area India ADM1 dataset collected by DataMeet India and the Election Commission of India. It uses a simplified local projection for interactive state selection.
